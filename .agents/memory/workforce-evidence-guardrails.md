---
name: Workforce evidence guardrails
description: User-confirmed constraints for interpreting and dating workforce trend evidence.
---

Only assert skill demand or a direction when the source directly supports that
specific capability. General AI adoption, occupation shortages, framework
competencies, and tool examples do not establish demand growth for every
related canonical skill. Conflicting sector signals must not be reduced to a
single unsupported overall direction. Fewer supported records are preferable
to filling a quota.

**Why:** The user repeated this constraint when asking to repair an evidence
deliverable whose trends file was still empty.

**How to apply:** Check the source's actual skill category, industry,
geography, observation period and forecast/observed distinction before assigning
a canonical label and direction. A contextual role link never verifies the
role profile or makes that skill universally mandatory.

Do not fabricate a publication day from a copyright year, an upload path or a
general study-launch date. Keep a record pending if an existing validation
contract requires metadata the specific source does not provide.

**Why:** An official study's public launch can be dated even when a particular
volume/version has no established publication day.

**How to apply:** Record the known precision in limitations and leave the date
blank rather than introducing false precision. Propose a separate contract
change if partial publication dates need to be supported.
