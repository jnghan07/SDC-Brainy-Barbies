##### Overview

**Job Market Insights** are quarterly releases based on the job offerings
advertised online through major private recruitment platforms. This report
contains keyinformation on job
offerings by major occupational groups
and economic
activities.

**Job Offering**
**Advertised Online** **,** **Fourth** **Quarter of 202** **5**

The
number of online job offerings advertised in the fourth
quarter of 2025 was 587,801.

**Job Offering Advertised**
**Online for Major Group Occupation**

Online job offerings were
concentrated in the Professional category, accounting for 46.4 per cent or
272,698 job offerings in the fourth quarter of 2025, compared to 268,178 job
offerings in the third quarter of 2025. Technicians and Associate Professionals
category recorded 93,358 job offerings, followed by Managers with 88,080 job
offerings. Higher demand was observed for Advertising and Marketing
Professionals (42,411 job offerings), followed byManaging
Directors and Chief Executives (36,656 job offerings), AdministrativeAssociate
Professionals (29,569 job offerings), Administrative Professionals (22,093 job
offerings), as well as Accountants and Auditors (20,111 job offerings).

**Job Offering Advertised**
**Online for Economic Activities**

The Services sector
continued to dominate, accounting for 84.4 per cent or 496,065 job offerings in
the fourth quarter of 2025, compared to 77.2 per cent in the third quarter of
2025\. The Wholesale and Retail Trade; Repair of Motor Vehicles and Motorcycles
subsector recorded the highest number of job offerings within the Services
sector, with 183,653 job offerings. This was followed by Professional,
Scientific and Technical Activities (62,954 job offerings), Administrative and
Support Service Activities (52,036 job offerings), Manufacturing (45,369 job
offerings), and the Construction sector (40,069 job offerings).
